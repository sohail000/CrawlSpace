Crawl pages that need JavaScript

Sites like YouTube build their pages with scripts, so Crawlspace can’t fetch them by address. This bookmark brings a page in exactly as your browser shows it.

Crawl in Crawlspace
  1. Drag the yellow button to your bookmarks bar. (Can’t see the bar? Press Ctrl+Shift+B, or ⌘+Shift+B on a Mac.)
  2. Open any page, like YouTube, and let it finish loading.
  3. Click the bookmark. The page opens here, ready for the spiders.
The page goes straight from your tab to this one. It doesn’t pass through any server.
